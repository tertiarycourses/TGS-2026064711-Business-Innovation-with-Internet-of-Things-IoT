# Hands-On Labs — IoT with Raspberry Pi

**WSQ Course Code:** TGS-2020505433 · Conducted by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd (UEN 201200696W)

All labs run on our IoT platform **IoTFlow** — https://iot.tertiaryinfotech.com — and are completed across Topics 2–4 (300 minutes of practical time over 2 days). Work through them in order; each lab builds on the one before.

Full step-by-step instructions are also in the Learner Guide. The slide deck shows each lab's purpose and outcome only.

## Topic 02 — Collect and Post Environmental Data to Cloud  (LO2 · A2, K2, K3 · Day 1 PM)

- [Lab 1: Register Your Raspberry Pi on IoTFlow](lab-01-register-your-raspberry-pi-on-iotflow.md) — A registered Raspberry Pi device in your own IoTFlow project, with its device token saved
- [Lab 2: Collect Environmental Data and Post It to the Cloud](lab-02-collect-environmental-data-and-post-it-to-the-cloud.md) — Live temperature and humidity telemetry streaming from the Raspberry Pi into IoTFlow

## Topic 03 — IoT Data Analytics and Visualization  (LO3 · A3, K4, K5 · Day 2 AM)

- [Lab 3: Analyse and Visualise Your IoT Data on the Cloud](lab-03-analyse-and-visualise-your-iot-data-on-the-cloud.md) — A live monitoring dashboard for your Raspberry Pi, plus a working threshold alert rule

## Topic 04 — Monitor Data to Trigger Control from Cloud  (LO4 · A4, K6, K7 · Day 2 PM)

- [Lab 4: Monitor Data and Trigger Control on the Raspberry Pi](lab-04-monitor-data-and-trigger-control-on-the-raspberry-pi.md) — A dashboard switch and slider that control an LED/relay on the Raspberry Pi in real time
