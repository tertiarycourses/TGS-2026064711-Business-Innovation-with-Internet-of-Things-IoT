# Hands-On Labs — Business Innovation with Internet-of-Things (IoT)

**WSQ Course Code:** TGS-2026064711 · Conducted by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd (UEN 201200696W)

All labs run on our IoT platform **IoTFlow** — https://iot.tertiaryinfotech.com — and are completed during the 90-minute practical block in Topic 3. Work through them in order; each lab builds on the one before.

Full step-by-step instructions are also in the Learner Guide. The slide deck shows each lab's purpose and outcome only.

## Topic 03 — IoT Technology  (LO3 · A3, K2 · 60 min + 90 min practical)

- [Lab 1: Register Your First IoT Device on IoTFlow](lab-01-register-your-first-iot-device-on-iotflow.md) — A registered IoT device in your own IoTFlow project, with its device token saved
- [Lab 2: Post Business Data to the Cloud (HTTP & MQTT)](lab-02-post-business-data-to-the-cloud-http-mqtt.md) — Live temperature and humidity telemetry streaming into IoTFlow from terminal and Python
- [Lab 3: Read Data Back and Remotely Control the Asset](lab-03-read-data-back-and-remotely-control-the-asset.md) — A dashboard switch and slider that control a relay/LED on the device in real time
- [Lab 4: Turn IoT Data into Business Insight](lab-04-turn-iot-data-into-business-insight.md) — A monitoring dashboard plus an alert-driven n8n workflow with an AI summary and a downlink command
